How grouping works +

Grouping compares complete extracted header fields and the detected body after collapsing whitespace and removing recognized source stamps. It does not strip reply prefixes, repair OCR, or merge similar subjects. Identical visible text with unresolved participants is only a possible repeat. Even groups with fuller headers remain unverified; they are not proof of a unique underlying message. Body and attachment boundaries need review.

Zero repeat groups means no matches under these strict rules; it does not mean all messages are unique. The review focus applies to groups; a source-document filter retains the group's other source references when copies span documents.
