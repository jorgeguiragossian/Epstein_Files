What these counts mean +

Candidate totals count source header occurrences, including quotes and wrappers; they are not unique messages. Reviewed documents have at least one reviewed candidate and may contain many unreviewed records. Boundary counts include only source-reviewed flags; unreviewed candidates have not been cleared. These flags cover quoted material, forwarding wrappers and incomplete continuations. A breakdown or adjacent-page check records evidence without clearing the original uncertainty.

Parser From/To gaps mean no syntactically detected address. Parser date gaps combine missing, ambiguous and unparsed dates; timestamps without timezones remain candidates but are not included in this gap count. Source readings do not overwrite native extraction. Zero detected candidates is not proof that a PDF contains no email, especially where text extraction is poor.

Email totals count candidate occurrences; brief totals count documents. Brief checks record a same-assistant editorial pass over statement support, attribution, page scope and limitations; drafts remain drafts. Date-only documents have a reviewed primary date but no prepared content brief. Email dashboard totals count candidates. Links open review groups that retain every source occurrence. If future identical-copy groups contain multiple candidates, a linked group count can differ from its dashboard candidate count.
