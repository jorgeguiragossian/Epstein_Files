Counting rules and limitations +

Dates are reviewed primary document dates, not the dates of every event or quoted email within a PDF. The date range is inclusive. Undated, uncertain and unreviewed documents are excluded even when no range is entered. A person filter counts only pages with a curated literal-name match, not all documents associated with that person or verified identities.

Words are lowercased English alphabetic tokens of at least three letters, with accents normalized. Common English words, email header terms, month/day names, recognized source-stamp and repeated transcript-vendor footer lines, URLs and email addresses are excluded. Digit-bearing IDs are omitted. Boilerplate, repeated quotations, OCR errors and duplicate material can affect counts; these are not unique-message frequencies, themes established by AI, or evidence of relationships.

Top terms sort by descending occurrences, then alphabetically for ties. The evidence list shows exact token counts by source page. Raw source text remains unchanged.
